# Voice

Use this for speech and notes, not for a new PDF. If setup is incomplete, stop and follow `docs/setup.md`.

Read the spoken section of `STYLE.md` first. Friendly and straightforward. First person. No em dashes. Do not open by saying what they are not. Facts come from `experience.md` and `candidate_profile.json`. Tenure follows the employment dates, not the job's years floor.

## Intro

They say "tell me about yourself", `/intro`, or ask for an opening pitch.

Write one spoken answer, about 60–90 seconds. Default audience is a recruiter. If they say hiring manager, add one concrete system detail.

1. Who they are: name, title, how long, the kind of system they own.
2. What they do: production work first, then the other thing they built. Plant three or four phrases from the job inside stories they can defend.
3. What led them here: that work points at a subject this company actually works on.
4. Why this role: the team and the work.

Skip any phrase they cannot defend. Deliver the speech, then a short map of which job phrases you used. Do not tailor a resume.

## Outreach

They want a short LinkedIn or hiring-manager note.

Ask about the other person's work. Then a few sentences of true proof. If they already applied, say that in the last sentence. Do not open with "I applied."

Pick one lane: SE, SWE, FDE, or GTM. If it is unclear, ask.

| Lane | The note is about | Do not |
|------|-------------------|--------|
| SE | Customer problem and showing value | Invent a quota or an SE title they did not hold |
| SWE | What they ship, and why this product | Reuse a sales-engineering pitch |
| FDE | Building with the customer | Sound like a touring demo role when the job is embed |
| GTM | Technical delivery, enablement, or partnerships | Invent CRM, quota, or pipeline numbers |

If they have not answered yet, ask and wait: who they are writing to, one true proof from `experience.md`, what they want to ask, and whether they already applied. Draft only from those answers. Keep their wording when it is already clear.

## Depth reply

Someone asked how they meet a posting, or a short connection note is the wrong shape. If this is a first connection note, use outreach instead.

1. How their work matches the way the role operates.
2. Requirements they meet, each tied to a section in `experience.md`.
3. Gaps on this posting, stated plainly, without a fix that invents experience.

A few short paragraphs they can paste.

## Recruiter questions

Write 7 questions a recruiter can answer, then 2 or 3 to save for the hiring manager.

Useful questions ask where the week goes, how this posting differs from a sibling req, what the level means, and what the process is. Skip culture questions and architecture questions the recruiter cannot know. Park architecture for the hiring manager.

If no job description is attached, ask which file. Do not invent the company's strategy.
