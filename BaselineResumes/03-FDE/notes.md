# 03-FDE

Production forward-deployed or applied engineer who builds inside a customer environment.

## What leads

1. Something they shipped that unblocked a customer or a delivery team.
2. The next build story with an observable outcome (reliability, data, API, evaluation).
3. How they learned the workflow: interviews, scope, or a working proof, after the build proof is already on the page.

## Skim test

Read the first six bullets. If they are mostly demos, decks, or enablement, this ordering is wrong. Use `02-SWE-Adjacent` for a solutions-shaped seat, or rebuild this page build-first.

## Do not

- Imply they held a forward-deployed title unless `experience.md` says so.
- Invent customer go-lives, connectors, or domains outside the claim limits.

## After setup

`/setup` reorders approved bullets. Thin customer-facing proof means this baseline stays honest and short on that theme, not padded.
