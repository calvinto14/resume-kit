# 02-SWE-Adjacent

Solutions engineer, solutions architect, sales engineer, or a solutions-shaped forward-deployed role. The page should show customer problem, then the thing they built or showed, then the outcome.

## What leads

1. Discovery, a demo, or a proof of concept that changed a decision.
2. The build that made that customer motion real.
3. Enablement, requirements, or scope they personally drove.
4. Production engineering as proof they can go deep, not as the headline, unless the posting is mostly engineering.

## Do not

- Claim a sales quota, a CRM, or a bookings number that is not in `experience.md`.
- Use this ordering for a production forward-deployed role that wants shipped systems first. That is `03-FDE`.

## After setup

`/setup` reorders the same approved bullets. It does not write new ones.
