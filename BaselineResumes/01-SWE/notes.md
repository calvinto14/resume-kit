# 01-SWE

Software engineer, individual contributor. Cold-send this ordering when the job is build, own, and ship.

## What leads

1. Production system they designed or built, with the stack and a defendable outcome.
2. The next strongest build story (API, data, reliability, or frontend) that matches the posting.
3. Ownership: design docs, reviews, or a system they actually owned.
4. Supporting build stories. Demos, interviews, and enablement stay in the bottom third unless the posting is about those.

## Do not

- Invent a domain, a title, or a metric that `experience.md` does not contain.
- Put a language list on the title line.
- Lead with a slide deck when the job is an engineering seat.

## After setup

`/setup` reorders approved bullets into `Resume.tex` here. Tailoring copies this file only as a starting order, then rewrites from `experience.md` for the specific job.
