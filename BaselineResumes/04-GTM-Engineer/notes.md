# 04-GTM-Engineer

Technical go-to-market, applications engineering, partnerships, or implementation-flavored GTM. The proof is delivery that made a product usable: tooling, enablement, proofs of concept, and the systems behind them.

## What leads

1. A system or tool that helped other people deliver.
2. A customer or partner working session with a concrete outcome.
3. The engineering underneath that motion.

## Do not

- Invent CRM admin, outbound tooling, quota, LTV, or a sales-cycle percentage.
- Retitle their job to a GTM title they did not hold.

## After setup

`/setup` fills `templates/archetypes/04-GTM-Engineer/pattern.tex` from the person's own resume. If the honest story is mostly product engineering, say so when a posting is a pure revenue-operations seat, and treat that application as a reach.
