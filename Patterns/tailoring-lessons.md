# Tailoring lessons

Read the principles on every tailor. Append a short log entry after each completed tailor. Do not invent a claim to satisfy a lesson.

## Principles

### Approved sources only
Every bullet traces to `experience.md` or `resumeSource.tex`. If a metric, tool, title, or domain is not written there, it is not on the PDF.

### Lexicon echo
If the job description names a token and the lexicon section maps it as `has`, put that token in Skills and in the proof bullet. A synonym in the bullet with the keyword only in Skills is a packaging miss. If the map says `missing`, do not echo the word.

### One bullet, one story
Do not repeat the same metric or narrative. Outcomes are this person's contribution, defensible in one interview sentence. A team mandate is not a personal metric.

### Swap the pattern, not the domain
Related work can be phrased in neutral pattern language. Do not imply they worked in a domain the claim limits forbid.

### Page shape
Header is phone, email, and LinkedIn. No city in the header. Section order is Experience, Skills, Projects, Education. Exactly one page, filled from approved stories. Bold at most two spans per bullet, three only when one span is a metric. Prefer the job's own tool names and the impact number. Do not bold filler nouns.

### Role families
`01-SWE` leads with production build. `02-SWE-Adjacent` leads with discovery, demos, and customer outcome for solutions-shaped roles. `03-FDE` is build-first client embed: if the top of the page is only demos and decks, it is the wrong archetype. `04-GTM-Engineer` is technical go-to-market, partnerships, or applications engineering. Do not invent quota, CRM, or pipeline numbers.

### Voice
Say "AI agents" with a scope. Do not write vague "AI-assisted" for the same work. Test-driven development is human-led. Lead with the cloud provider the story actually used. Languages go in Skills and inside bullets, not after the job title.
